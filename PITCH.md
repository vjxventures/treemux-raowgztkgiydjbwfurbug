# AgentProbe: The Reliability Layer for AI Agents in Production

As AI agents move from demos to production - executing multi-step workflows, making API calls, and handling real customer interactions - the biggest bottleneck isn't capability, it's reliability. AgentProbe is the observability and testing platform purpose-built for AI agents: think Datadog meets testing infrastructure, designed for non-deterministic systems. The platform provides continuous evaluation with regression testing suites, real-time trace visualization with step-by-step waterfall analysis, sandbox simulation for adversarial and chaos testing, and automated alerting on reliability regressions - giving engineering teams the confidence to ship agents that enterprises will trust with mission-critical workflows. By embedding into CI/CD pipelines and development workflows, AgentProbe becomes a standard dependency in the AI deployment stack, capturing developer-first adoption before expanding into enterprise compliance and audit requirements.
